About the Event

India’s Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) represents the world’s most ambitious rural drinking water initiative, having connected over 150 million rural households to Functional Household Tap Connections (FHTC). However, transitioning from rapid capital expenditure to multi-decadal sustainability demands a fundamental paradigm shift.

Ensuring uninterrupted water quality, reliable energy sources, financial cost recovery, and climate resilience across varied hydrogeological terrains requires empowered Village Water & Sanitation Committees (VWSCs) and robust technical-administrative backstopping.

plumbing Pillar 01

Infrastructure to Functionality

Moving beyond physical pipeline installation toward uninterrupted daily supply, 55 LPCD potable water standards, and real-time sensorized water testing at the tap.

supervised_user_circle Pillar 02

Community-Led Governance

Empowering Gram Panchayats and Paani Samitis with financial accounting skills, transparent user-fee collection, and active leadership from women village water managers.

eco Pillar 03

Climate & Source Resilience

Integrating participatory groundwater recharge, watershed conservation, and solarized pumping arrays to safeguard critical water sources against climate-induced drought shocks.

Purpose of the Event

This global session converges frontline practitioners, multilateral donors, and policy architects to distill lessons from India’s rural water transformation and translate them into replicable frameworks for the Global South.

Target 6.1 Availability & Safety

Universal, affordable, and bacterially safe drinking water for every household.

Target 6.5 Operation & Maintenance

Sustainable institutional O&M architecture at sub-district and village levels.

Target 6.A Digital & Evidence

IoT-based water monitoring, digital billing, and transparent grievance redressal.

Target 6.B Policy Dialogue

Elevating grassroots operational realities into UN Water Conference 2026 mandates.

Relevant Material: Jal-Suraksha: Securing Rural Drinking Water Services

A Robust Operation & Maintenance Framework for the Jal Jeevan Mission at the Gram Panchayat

Operational manual synthesised by WOTR and W-CReS outlining Gram Panchayat pathways for zero-downtime rural tap infrastructure.

1
Institutional Mechanisms & Governance

Roles and operational guidelines for Gram Panchayats, VWSCs, Pani Samitis, and local water management bodies.

2
Technical Operations & Infrastructure Maintenance

Preventive maintenance protocols, asset registers, leak detection, water pump/pipeline repairs, and spare part management.

3
Water Quality Monitoring & Surveillance

Regular field testing kit (FTK) usage, chlorination, sanitary inspections, and testing via accredited laboratories.

4
Financial Sustainability & Tariff Recovery

Transparent user fee structures, O&M costing frameworks, emergency contingency funds, and public subsidy integration.

5
Source Sustainability & Community Stewardship

Groundwater aquifer recharge, water security planning, source protection, and women-led community surveillance groups.
